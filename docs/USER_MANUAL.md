# Solo Leveling System - Complete User Manual

## 📱 Getting Started

### First Launch
1. Open the app in your browser (works on mobile and desktop)
2. Wait for the "Initializing System..." screen to complete (usually 2-5 seconds)
3. If the loading screen persists, wait up to 10 seconds - it will automatically hide
4. You'll see a welcome notification: "[System] Initialization Complete"

### Navigation
The app has 7 main pages accessible via the bottom navigation bar:
- ⚡ **Dashboard** - Overview of your stats, XP, and active quests
- 📋 **Quests** - View and manage all your quests
- 🌟 **Skills** - Skill tree, active skills, passive skills, crafting, and dungeons
- 📊 **Stats** - Detailed statistics and analytics
- 🏆 **Achievements** - Streaks, challenges, and unlocked achievements
- 🤖 **AI** - Journal, quest reports, and purchase reports
- 🛒 **Shop** - Real-world shop for books, courses, and tools

---

## 🎯 Core Gameplay

### How the System Works
This is a **real-world leveling system** inspired by Solo Leveling. Unlike games, everything you do in real life can help you level up.

### The Flow:
1. **Share Your Life** → Go to AI tab, write about your day, experiences, goals
2. **System Analyzes** → AI analyzes your input and generates personalized quests
3. **Complete Quests** → Do real-world actions (study, exercise, network, etc.)
4. **Report Results** → Tell the system what you accomplished
5. **Get Rewards** → System automatically evaluates and rewards you with XP, stats, items

---

## 📋 Quest System

### Types of Quests

#### 1. **Daily Quests**
- Reset every day
- Must be completed or you'll get penalty quests
- Examples: "Read for 30 minutes", "Exercise for 20 minutes"

#### 2. **Weekly Quests**
- Longer-term objectives
- Reset every week
- Examples: "Complete a project", "Learn a new skill"

#### 3. **Emergency Quests**
- Time-limited urgent missions
- Appear randomly
- High rewards but must be completed quickly

#### 4. **Penalty Quests**
- Automatically generated if you fail daily quests
- Have negative consequences if not completed
- Examples: "Complete 2 extra daily quests", "Lose 50 XP"

#### 5. **Quest Chains**
- Multi-part quests that unlock progressively
- Complete one part to unlock the next
- Higher rewards for completing entire chains

### How to Complete Quests

1. **Accept a Quest**
   - Go to Quests page
   - Click on any quest card
   - Click "Accept Quest"

2. **Do the Real-World Action**
   - The quest tells you what to do
   - Example: "Read Chapter 3 of 'The Art of War'"
   - Go do it in real life!

3. **Report Your Completion**
   - Go to AI tab → Quest Reports tab
   - Select the quest from dropdown
   - Describe what you did, what happened, what you learned
   - Include details: money earned, items acquired, people met, etc.
   - Click "Submit Report"

4. **Get Rewards**
   - System analyzes your report
   - Automatically awards XP, stats, items
   - Shows a Solo Leveling-style reward screen

## 📊 Stats & Leveling

### Your 8 Core Stats
- **💪 Strength** - Physical power, exercise, fitness
- **🧠 Intelligence** - Learning, studying, knowledge
- **🎯 Strategy** - Planning, tactics, decision-making
- **🛡️ Endurance** - Stamina, persistence, resilience
- **✨ Wisdom** - Experience, insight, judgment
- **🤝 Social** - Relationships, networking, communication
- **⚕️ Medical** - Health knowledge, first aid, wellness
- **💰 Financial** - Money management, investing, earning

### Leveling Up
- Gain **XP (Experience Points)** by completing quests
- When you level up:
  - You get **Stat Points** to manually allocate
  - You get **Skill Points** for the skill tree
  - Your rank may increase (E → D → C → B → A → S → SS → SSS)

### Stat Point Allocation
When you level up:
1. A modal appears automatically
2. You see how many stat points you earned
3. Click the **+** button next to any stat to allocate a point
4. Points are distributed immediately
5. Close the modal when done

You can also allocate points later:
- Go to **Skills** or **Stats** page
- Look for "Unallocated Stat Points" section
- Click **+** buttons to distribute points

## 🌟 Skills System

### Skill Tree
- Located on the **Skills** page
- Spend **Skill Points** to unlock skills
- Skills provide permanent bonuses
- Some skills unlock other skills (prerequisites)

### Active Skills
- Skills you can activate manually
- Have cooldowns and MP (Focus) costs
- Examples:
  - **Deep Focus** - Increases learning speed for 1 hour
  - **Social Boost** - Enhances networking ability for 30 minutes
  - **Energy Surge** - Restores energy quickly

**How to Use:**
1. Go to Skills page
2. Scroll to "Active Skills" section
3. Click on a skill
4. Click "Activate" (if cooldown is ready)
5. Skill activates and shows countdown timer

### Passive Skills
- Automatically active all the time
- Provide continuous benefits
- Can be upgraded with skill points
- Examples:
  - **Auto Heal** - Slowly regenerates energy over time
  - **Danger Sense** - Alerts you to potential threats
  - **Energy Conservation** - Reduces energy consumption
  - **Focus Recovery** - Regenerates focus faster

**How to Upgrade:**
1. Go to Skills page
2. Scroll to "Passive Skills" section
3. Click "Upgrade" on any passive skill
4. Costs skill points

### Skill Books
- Items that unlock new active skills
- Can be purchased from shop or earned as rewards
- Use them from your inventory to unlock skills

## ⚡ Energy & Focus System

### Energy (HP)
- Represents your physical and mental energy
- Depletes when you do activities
- Regenerates over time (especially with passive skills)
- Low energy = harder to complete quests

### Focus (MP)
- Represents your mental focus/concentration
- Required to use active skills
- Regenerates faster than energy
- Can be boosted with skills

**View Energy/Focus:**
- Dashboard page shows bars at the top
- Blue bar = Energy
- Purple bar = Focus

## 🤖 AI Journal & Memory System

### Journal Tab
**Purpose:** Share your life experiences, goals, challenges with the AI

**How to Use:**
1. Go to AI tab
2. Make sure "Journal" tab is selected (default)
3. Type or speak about:
   - Your day
   - Life experiences
   - Goals
   - Challenges
   - Anything you want the system to remember
4. Click "Save Entry"

**Features:**
- **Offline Mode:** Text input works offline
- **Online Mode:** Voice input available (click 🎤 button)
- **Search:** Use search bar to find past entries
- **Filters:** Filter by category, sentiment, analyzed/unanalyzed

**What Happens:**
- System stores your entry
- When online, AI analyzes it
- Generates personalized quests based on your life
- Stores quests in "Pending Quests" for later delivery

### Quest Reports Tab
**Purpose:** Report how you completed quests in real life

**How to Use:**
1. Go to AI tab → Quest Reports tab
2. Select a quest from dropdown
3. Describe:
   - What you did
   - What happened
   - What you learned
   - Results (money earned, items acquired, people met, etc.)
4. Click "Submit Report"

**What Happens:**
- System analyzes your report
- Evaluates your real-world achievements
- Automatically awards XP, stats, items
- Shows reward screen

### Purchases Tab
**Purpose:** Report real-world purchases that help you level up

**How to Use:**
1. Go to AI tab → Purchases tab
2. Select purchase type (Book, Course, Tool, etc.)
3. Enter item name
4. Enter cost (optional)
5. Describe how it helps you level up
6. Click "Report Purchase"

**What Happens:**
- Item added to your inventory
- System tracks how it helps your growth
- May provide stat bonuses

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Loading Screen Stuck
**Problem:** App stuck on "Initializing System..." screen

**Solutions:**
1. Wait up to 10 seconds (safety timeout will hide it)
2. Refresh the page
3. Clear browser cache
4. Check browser console for errors (F12)
5. Try a different browser

### Quest Not Appearing
**Problem:** Quest you expected isn't showing

**Solutions:**
1. Check "Pending Quests" - may be scheduled for later
2. Go to AI tab and share more about your life
3. Complete existing quests first
4. Check if you have too many active quests

### Stats Not Updating
**Problem:** Stats don't change after completing quest

**Solutions:**
1. Make sure you submitted a quest report
2. Check the reward screen - stats should show there
3. Refresh the page
4. Check Stats page - may need to scroll

### Skills Not Working
**Problem:** Active skill doesn't activate

**Solutions:**
1. Check if cooldown is ready
2. Check if you have enough Focus (MP)
3. Make sure skill is unlocked in skill tree
4. Try refreshing the page

### Offline Mode Issues
**Problem:** Features not working offline

**Solutions:**
1. Journal (text) works offline
2. Voice input requires internet
3. Quest generation requires internet (but cached quests work offline)
4. Most features work offline once loaded
