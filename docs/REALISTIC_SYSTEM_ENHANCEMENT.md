## 📋 EXAMPLE QUEST STRUCTURE

### Before (Abstract):
"Apply the principle to identify and influence a key leverage point."

### After (Real-World):
```
[REAL-WORLD APPLICATION REQUIRED]

Principle: "Control key terrain"
Source: The Art of War by Sun Tzu

YOUR MISSION:
REAL-WORLD ACTION: Identify ONE specific leverage point in your current 
environment (workplace, school, community, or home). Write down: 
(1) What makes it a leverage point, 
(2) One concrete action you will take this week to influence it, 
(3) How you'll measure the impact. 
Then execute the action and document the result.

REPORT REQUIREMENTS:
- Describe the real situation you chose
- Explain how you applied the principle
- Detail the specific action you took
- Report the measurable outcome or result
- Reflect on what you learned

This quest requires real-world action, not theoretical discussion.
```

## 🔍 VERIFICATION CRITERIA

### Required Elements:
1. **Real-World Action** ✅
   - Must use action words: "applied", "executed", "took action"
   - Must show actual doing, not just thinking

2. **Real-World Context** ✅
   - Must mention specific place: "at work", "at school", "with my team"
   - Must reference real people or situations
   - Must be concrete, not abstract

3. **Measurable Outcome** ✅
   - Must report what happened
   - Must show results or impact
   - Must demonstrate completion

4. **Reflection** ✅ (Bonus)
   - Shows learning
   - Demonstrates understanding
   - Indicates growth
